        <h2 class="section-title">The 4 Costliest Misconceptions About IT Services</h2>

        <div class="misconception">
            <h3 class="misconception-title">Misconception #1: My network doesn't need 24/7 security monitoring</h3>
            <p>This is probably one of the biggest and costliest misconceptions that business owners have. Usually this is because they've been fortunate enough to never experience a ransomware attack – but that's similar to thinking you don't need to wear a seat belt because you've never had an accident.</p>
            <p><strong>Modern cybersecurity threats don't wait for business hours.</strong> Ransomware attacks happen at 2 AM. Hackers probe your defenses 24/7. Data breaches occur on weekends. Without continuous monitoring, you won't know you've been compromised until it's too late.</p>
            <p>If your IT company doesn't insist on 24/7 security monitoring with threat detection, DO NOT HIRE THEM. Either they don't understand modern cybersecurity (which is frightening), OR they're profiting from cleaning up disasters instead of preventing them.</p>
        </div>

        <div class="misconception">
            <h3 class="misconception-title">Misconception #2: My nephew/neighbor/office manager can handle our IT and cybersecurity</h3>
            <p>Most people look for a part-time "guru" for one reason: to save money. But this often comes back to haunt them catastrophically. We frequently get calls from business owners who desperately need our help after an inexperienced friend or relative caused a security breach or couldn't stop a ransomware attack.</p>
            <p>Cybersecurity is not a hobby. It requires specialized knowledge, certifications, experience, and constant training to stay ahead of evolving threats. Do you really want a part-time amateur responsible for protecting your customer data, financial records, and business reputation? As with everything in life, you get what you pay for.</p>
        </div>

        <div class="misconception">
            <h3 class="misconception-title">Misconception #3: All IT companies are created equal – choose the cheapest</h3>
            <p>A cheap price usually means cheap security. Really good cybersecurity professionals do NOT work cheap because they are in high demand. The only technicians that work cheap are those who are just starting out and are grossly inexperienced.</p>
            <p>An inexperienced IT company can cost you far more because:</p>
            <ul>
                <li>They improperly configure security controls, leaving you vulnerable</li>
                <li>They take 3-5 times longer to fix problems an experienced team would solve quickly</li>
                <li>They could cause a data breach or fail to prevent ransomware, costing you hundreds of thousands</li>
            </ul>
            <p>With your client data, financial records, and business reputation at stake, do you REALLY want the lowest-priced company protecting you?</p>
        </div>

            <h3 class="misconception-title">Misconception #4: An honest IT company should quote prices over the phone</h3>
            <p>I wish this were true, but it isn't. Just like a good doctor, an honest IT professional needs to assess your environment before quoting prices. Every network is different, every security posture is unique, and every compliance requirement varies.</p>

        <h2 class="section-title">5 Critical Mistakes to Avoid When Choosing an IT Company</h2>

        <div class="misconception">
            <h3 class="misconception-title">Mistake #1: Choosing based on a single phone call</h3>
            <p>We recommend inviting them to your office for a proper security assessment. A competent professional should offer to audit your environment before quoting anything. Prescription without diagnosis is malpractice – whether in medicine or IT security.</p>
        </div>

        <div class="misconception">
            <h3 class="misconception-title">Mistake #2: Choosing a company without a satisfaction guarantee</h3>
            <p>A good IT services firm should stand behind their work. If they can't fix a problem to YOUR satisfaction, you shouldn't get stuck with the bill. The fact that they offer a guarantee shows confidence in their ability to deliver results.</p>
        </div>

        <div class="misconception">
            <h3 class="misconception-title">Mistake #3: Not checking references thoroughly</h3>
            <p>Don't just take the sales person's word. Ask to speak to at least 3-4 current clients similar to your business size and industry. If they hesitate or can't provide references, that's a massive red flag. Check online reviews, testimonials, and case studies.</p>
        </div>

        <div class="misconception">
            <h3 class="misconception-title">Mistake #4: Choosing a company without cybersecurity expertise</h3>
            <p>In 2025, you cannot separate IT from cybersecurity. They're one and the same. If an IT company doesn't specialize in security – with certifications, SIEM capabilities, compliance expertise, and documented security processes – they're living in the past and putting your business at risk.</p>
        </div>

        <div class="misconception">
            <h3 class="misconception-title">Mistake #5: Choosing a company that doesn't understand your compliance requirements</h3>
            <p>If you handle sensitive data, accept payments, or work in a regulated industry, compliance isn't optional. SOC 2, FTC Safeguards, PCI-DSS, HIPAA – these aren't just acronyms, they're legal requirements with massive fines for violations. Your IT company must have proven compliance expertise.</p>
        </div>
