REASON-NOW HOOKS (pick one)

Insurance pressure: “Do you know your policy’s minimum requirements—and can your IT prove you meet them?”

Account takeover: “If a mailbox gets compromised today, what’s the time-to-containment—and who executes it?”

OUTBOUND CALL (90 seconds)

Primary goal: Book the FTA (15–30 min) and deliver a Security Reality Snapshot.
Fallback: If they won’t book, offer coffee/lunch as the low-pressure version of the same conversation (still to qualify + set next step).

Opener + flow

“Hi <NAME>, this is Joe with Digerati Experts. I’ll be brief—do you handle IT internally or do you use an outside provider?”

If outside provider: “Totally fine—this isn’t a rip-and-replace. I do quick second-opinion checks focused on identity, backups, and evidence.”

“Can I ask two quick questions to see if an FTA is even worth it?”

Qualifying questions (use 2–4)

“Are you on Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace—and is MFA enforced for everyone (including admins)?”

“When was your last successful restore test (not just ‘we have backups’)?”

“If a mailbox gets compromised today, who contains it and what are the first 5 actions?”

“If you had to defend yourself—could you produce logs and a timeline of access/activity?”

“If someone leaves today, can you prove they lose access everywhere within 15 minutes?”

Close

FTA: “Let’s do a 15-minute FTA. If I don’t find anything meaningful, I’ll tell you—and we’ll leave it there.”
Coffee fallback: “If you’d rather keep it informal, let’s grab coffee. I’ll ask a few questions and tell you what matters and what doesn’t.”

COFFEE MEETING (30 minutes)

Goal: qualify + trust + clear next step (FTA or Assessment).
Agenda: 1) business context 2) current IT reality 3) insurance/incident readiness 4) next step

Questions

“What’s driving you to take this meeting right now?”

“What would a good outcome look like 90 days from today?”

“What’s the biggest recurring tech pain—and what’s the business impact?”

“Any insurance renewals, questionnaires, or compliance pressure coming up?”

“Have you ever had an email compromise, wire attempt, ransomware scare, or vendor fraud?”

“Who owns decisions—who signs, and who runs day-to-day?”

“Cheapest possible, or reliable and defensible?”

FTA (15–30 minutes)

Purpose: confirm basics + surface gaps + set next step
Deliverable: Security Reality Snapshot (plain English)

Talk track

Context (2–3 min): business, locations, user count, critical apps, downtime impact

Identity (5 min): M365/Google, MFA enforcement, admin separation, offboarding speed

Backups (5 min): what’s backed up, last restore test, immutability, access separation

Evidence (3–5 min): logging + ability to reconstruct timeline

Direction (2 min): 1–3 priority gaps + next step recommendation

PHONE-READY Q&A (short answers)

Q: So what do you guys do?
A: “We help small businesses understand their real cyber risk, put the right security controls in place, and be able to prove what happened when something goes wrong. We deliver that through managed security, identity, cloud systems, backup and recovery, and IT operations built around how your business actually works.”

Q: How long have you been doing this?
A: “I’ve been doing IT since 2007. DE is security-first—controls and evidence, not just fixing issues.”

Q: Do you do IT and cyber, or just cyber?
A: “Both. Security-first IT. Fully managed or co-managed security.”

Q: What’s the first step?
A: “A free security risk assessment. If it’s a fit, the first paid step is onboarding and stabilization.”

Q: How much do you cost?
A: “A few thousand to get started, then a few hundred to a few thousand per month depending on users, risk, and what you actually need.”

Q: Why should we switch? We already have IT.
A: “You don’t have to. This is a second opinion. Can they prove restore tests, lock down identity fast, and produce incident evidence?”

Q: What tools do you use?
A: “Identity, endpoint, email security, secure access, backups, and logging—chosen to fit the workflow. No forced bundle.”

Q: What’s included monthly vs extra?
A: “Monthly includes IT support + cyber/security requests, problems, and incidents—and support for anything we sold you. Out-of-scope is paid-helpdesk support.”

Q: Do you support things you didn’t sell?
A: “We support what we sell in-package. For third-party stuff, we can help via paid-helpdesk time or a scoped project so it’s supportable.”

Q: What’s TechPoints?
A: “Credits you can earn and apply to paid-helpdesk time. If TechPoints exist, we use them first.”

Q: How fast are your SLAs?
A: “Security-impacting issues and lockouts get priority. Exact targets are documented in the agreement.”

Q: How big is your team?
A: “Lean and accountable, with a vetted bench for coverage—no single point of failure.”

Q: What happens if you get sick or die?
A: “You won’t be stuck. Clients retain owner-level access, and we maintain a documented handoff package stored in secure credential escrow so another provider can take over quickly.”

Q: Can we leave anytime and still get our stuff?
A: “Yes. Your systems and data are yours. We provide an exportable handoff package and transition assistance.”

Q: How do you handle clients who decline protections?
A: “We explain tradeoffs plainly. If protections are declined, we document risk acceptance so everyone is clear.”

Q: What does credential escrow mean?
A: “A secure break-glass package the client controls—critical access + restore steps—so no one gets locked out.”

Q: What does transition assistance mean?
A: “Standard export + handoff package and a defined transition window so you’re not disrupted.”

Q: Do you require client-owned access?
A: “Yes—clients retain owner/admin access for core systems. It prevents hostage risk and makes continuity defensible.”
