Section title

Objection Handlers (Use verbatim on calls)

1️⃣ “We already have IT.”

Response

Totally fine. This isn’t a rip-and-replace. It’s a second opinion focused on identity, backups, and evidence. 
If your current provider can prove restore tests, lock down access fast, and produce incident evidence, great—you’ll confirm it. If not, you’ll know exactly what to fix.

2️⃣ “We’re under contract.”

Response

Understood. Contracts don’t stop audits or incidents. 
We can still do a quick Security Reality Snapshot so you know where you stand—no disruption, no replacement.

3️⃣ “Just send me information.”

Response

Happy to—but it’ll be more relevant after a quick 15-minute FTA. 
I don’t send generic brochures. When’s a good time for a short slot?

4️⃣ “We’re too busy.”

Response

That’s exactly why we keep the first step short—15 minutes now can save hours later. 
If timing is tight, we can do coffee as the low-pressure version.

5️⃣ “Price is the main thing.”

Response

If you’re shopping for the cheapest IT, we’re probably not the right fit.
If you want clarity, risk reduction, and a provider that owns outcomes, we’re usually very reasonable.

6️⃣ “Can you just help with this one small thing?”

Response

Yes—if it fits a defined scope and doesn’t create unmanaged risk.
If it’s security-related, we’ll either do it as a small project or roll it into onboarding so it’s supported properly.

7️⃣ “We don’t need all the security stuff.”

Response

That’s fine—we’ll explain the tradeoffs.
If certain protections are declined, we document them as a risk acceptance so everyone’s clear on ownership and expectations.

8️⃣ “Our current IT says we’re covered.”

Response

That may be true. The question is whether they can prove it—
restore tests, access containment, and incident evidence.
We help validate that, not argue opinions.

9️⃣ “We’ve never had a breach.”

Response

Most companies haven’t—until they do.
Our focus is making sure that if something happens, you can respond quickly and prove what happened.

🔟 “We’re small—we’re not a target.”

Response

Most incidents at this size are automated—phishing, account takeover, and credential abuse.
Attackers don’t target companies; they target weak identity and access.

1️⃣1️⃣ “We just want IT support, not security.”

Response

We do IT—but security-first.
We include IT support and cyber together so you don’t have gaps between ‘it works’ and ‘it’s defensible.’

1️⃣2️⃣ “What if we don’t want to follow your standards?”

Response

We’re upfront about minimum standards like MFA and restore testing.
If those are declined, we document the risk and may limit scope—we won’t silently accept unmanaged risk.

Avoids repetitive “we already have IT” variants

Keeps tone calm, confident, not defensive

Aligns perfectly with:

FTA-first motion

Security-first positioning

Risk acceptance framework

Client-owned access principles
