Should It Be a 2-Step Form?
YES - absolutely recommend a 2-step approach. Here's why and how:
Why 2-Step Works Better:

Lower initial commitment - Name + Email feels like "just getting info," not "signing up for a sales call"
Higher completion rates - Studies show 2-step forms convert 20-30% better than single long forms
Qualification happens after interest - Once they've committed with email, they're more likely to complete step 2
Feels like progress - People who complete step 1 feel invested and want to finish

How to Structure It:
Step 1 (Current hero form):

Full Name
Email Address
Button: "Get Free Assessment →"

Step 2 (Modal or slide-in after submission):
Shows a success message like "Great! Just 3 more questions to personalize your assessment..."
Then collect:

Phone Number (optional or required depending on your sales process)
Company Name
How many endpoints/devices? (dropdown: 1-10, 11-25, 26-50, 51-100, 100+)
Do you currently have an MSP/IT provider? (Yes / No / Looking to switch)
Primary concern: (dropdown: Compliance requirements, Cyber insurance, Recent breach/threat, General protection, Other)

Optional final question:

When would you like your assessment? (This week, Next week, Within 30 days) - helps with lead routing

Visual Treatment for Step 2:
Instead of navigating to a new page, use a modal overlay or slide-in panel that appears immediately after they submit step 1. This keeps them in the flow and feels like a natural progression, not a bait-and-switch.
Show a progress indicator (Step 2 of 2) and keep it short - 3-4 questions max. Make it feel like they're almost done.
Alternative: Progressive Disclosure on Same Page
If you don't want a modal, you could also:

Fade out the original form
Slide in the new questions below with a smooth animation
Show "Step 1 Complete ✓" with their email
"Just 3 more questions to customize your assessment"

This approach is slightly less aggressive than a modal but still keeps them on the page.

Bottom line: Yes, go 2-step. Get the email first with minimal friction, then qualify them when they're already committed. You'll get way more leads and still get the qualification data you need for sales.
