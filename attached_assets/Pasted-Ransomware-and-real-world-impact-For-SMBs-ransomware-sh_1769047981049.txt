“Even larger organizations aren’t immune: ransomware is a component of 39% of breaches.” (Verizon DBIR 2025 Executive Summary.)

“Average ransomware recovery cost (excluding ransom) was $1.53M.” (Sophos State of Ransomware 2025.)

“53% of ransomware victims fully recovered within a week.” (Sophos State of Ransomware 2025.)

“49% of ransomware victims paid to get their data back.” (Sophos State of Ransomware 2025.)

“Backups were used to restore encrypted data in 54% of incidents (lowest rate in six years).” (Sophos State of Ransomware 2025.)
