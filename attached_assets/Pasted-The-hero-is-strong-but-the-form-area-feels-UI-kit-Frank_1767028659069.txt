The hero is strong, but the form area feels “UI-kit Frankenstein” right now.

The 5 things that still need to change (in order)
1) The “trust badges” row looks like a navigation bar (and it fights the form)

Right now those three items feel like clickable tabs inside the form, not credibility signals.

Fix

Move that row outside the form fields (either above the form as “Trusted by”, or below the CTA).

Restyle them to be small + light: no big pill background, no thick borders, no dividers.

Use either logos or simple text chips.

Example layout

Trusted by: [Microsoft Partner] [Apple Consultants] [SOC 2 vendors]

2) You have two CTAs competing (Get Free Assessment vs phone button)

The phone button is strong enough that it splits attention with your primary CTA.

Fix

Keep one primary CTA (the purple button).

Make the phone option a text link under it:
“Prefer to call? 325-480-9870”
or a smaller ghost button (less height, less glow).

3) The button icon alignment feels wrong

Your arrow looks like it’s floating off to the right instead of feeling like part of the button label.

Fix

Center the button content as a group: “Get Free Assessment →” (inline icon, 8–10px gap).

Don’t space the icon to the far edge unless the whole button is designed as “text left / icon right” (yours isn’t).

4) The form card glow/border is heavier than everything else

That purple outline/glow makes the card look like a modal or highlighted error state.

Fix

Reduce glow intensity and blur.

Use a subtler 1px border + soft shadow so it matches the feature pills and the dashboard style.

5) Spacing + rhythm: the microcopy and the stats feel disconnected

The “No obligation / Results in 24–48hrs / No credit card” is good, but it’s sitting too low and feels like a footer.
The big stat cards below feel like a new section crashing into the hero.

Fix

Pull the microcopy closer to the CTA (it should support the click).

Add a bit more vertical spacing between the form and the stat cards, or shrink the stat cards slightly.
