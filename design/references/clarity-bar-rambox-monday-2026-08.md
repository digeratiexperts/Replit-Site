# Clarity bar — Rambox & Monday (principles only)

Captured 2026-08-12 at 1440. Screenshots stay in `layouts/` for internal study. Do **not** copy their brand, layout, headlines, or “Book a demo” patterns into Digerati.

## What they do that we don’t (yet)

1. **One idea per band.** Hero is a claim + one product visual. The next band is a different idea. We stack four identical stat cards, then six identical tackle cards, then six identical capability cards — the page reads as a template, not a composition.
2. **Type hierarchy, not volume.** One large headline, quieter body, one primary button. Our section H2s jump to `text-5xl` / `text-6xl` and body to `text-2xl`, so nothing is quieter than anything else.
3. **Whitespace is structural.** Related items group; unrelated bands get air. We fill every band with card chrome and radial blobs.
4. **Imagery is the product/system.** Rambox shows the workspace; Monday shows the board. Clip-art and decorative orbs are absent. Our sculptures belong on the three engage paths; repeating a lattice next to four stat cards, or putting Lucide in six boxed tiles, dilutes that.
5. **One primary action.** Header CTA matches the hero CTA. Secondary is a ghost. We still have competing treatments on inner pages; homepage chrome must stay one brand gradient.
6. **Mobile is recomposed.** They stack a real column (claim → visual → list). Shrinking a 3×2 card grid is not a mobile composition.

## Apply in Digerati language

- Near-black / graphite field; violet as light, not paint.
- Sculptures only on engage-path cards (and nowhere else on the homepage).
- Small chrome = Lucide, not 3D toys, not rainbow tiles.
- Stats = number strip, not four cards.
- Tackle / capabilities / protect stack = lists inside a composed field, not another card grid.
- Process = numbered sequence, not four boxed twins.
- Kill decorative radial blobs and floating dots.
- Canonical CTA: **Get My Cyber Risk Assessment**.
