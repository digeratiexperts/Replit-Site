sophisticated, modern, cybersecurity-aware, highly engineered, culturally

3. **Premium underground record shop / listening room** — "tactile culture,
   independent identity, printed matter, blackened metal, purposeful objects,

### 3. The energy curve

The one moment, their seed, verbatim: **"THE ENVIRONMENT WAKES UP."** "Show a
fragmented company technology environment. Different systems are visibly

Connections form. … Disconnected technology becomes an operating ecosystem. The
visitor should understand something without requiring a paragraph: DE doesn't
just manage products. DE makes the environment work together."

### 5. One thing this site should do that no site they have seen does

Their seed, verbatim: **"THE DE TECHNOLOGY MAP.** The visitor can explore a
business environment through domains. Selecting an outcome changes which parts
of the technology environment activate." (Outcomes given: "Protect my
business", "Make employees more productive", "Automate my company", "Meet a
compliance requirement", each with the domain list that should highlight.) "Do
not force this concept if Scrollcraft discovers a better signature move. But
the finished site must have a real signature behavior."

### 6. Distance from premium-minimal

Their aesthetic instruction: "premium editorial web design, precise grids where
appropriate, intentionally broken grids where beneficial, extremely strong type
hierarchy, selective oversized typography, dramatic whitespace, physical-feeling

than everywhere, atmospheric depth, restrained glass, subtle noise/material
texture." Avoid: "gratuitous rounded cards, excessive gradients, generic SaaS
UI, bubblegum colors, random glassmorphism, cheap neon cyberpunk."

→ Family: **editorial** (uniqueness.md §5), carried with premium restraint.
Not premium-minimal-by-default; not maximalist.

### 7. One unbroken world, or distinct scenes?

Verbatim: "I do NOT want the entire website to become a single 3D scene that

→ **Distinct scenes.** Worldflight is off the table by client instruction.

```
1  Calm         a title page: warm paper, one enormous statement, nothing else asking for attention
2  Unease       their own stack named plainly on a hard cut to graphite; the margin map scatters as the list grows
3  Awe    PEAK  the scattered marks converge: one system draws itself across a full dark spread and wakes up
4  Agency       the map answers the visitor: choose an outcome, the domains that deliver it light up
5  Confidence   the same system shown operated: designed, monitored, measured, improved, on cadence
6  Depth        the range beyond an MSP: business systems, automation, AI as one contained domain, the marketplace
```

No two adjacent chapters share a feeling. The administrative facts (tiers,
contact) are compressed into flow, not pinned.

## Step 2 record: grammar, gate, score

### Grammar: chaptered editorial (uniqueness.md §2.2)

Why the other seven lost, against this interview:

1. **Filmic one-shot** forbids visible sequence and chapter chrome; the client
   mandated distinct chapters and "a premium editorial publication." It also
   carries the burden of proof as the house default.
2. **Live surface** forbids editorial display type and marketing chrome; this
   brief demands large editorial statements, and DE governance forbids anything
   that could read as a fake dashboard.
3. **Continuous world** is rejected verbatim by the client (§11 of the
   directive: "NOT one continuous fly-through world").
4. **Typographic poster** forbids photographic ground; the directive and DE
   governance both insist on real photography and real artifacts.
5. **Gallery/catalog** forbids the argument shape; this page IS an argument
   (fragmentation becomes one operating environment).
6. **Split stage** holds one two-sided comparison for the whole page; this
   journey has nine distinct movements that do not decompose into before/after.
7. **Rhythmic cutlist** is a pulse grammar; the client's energy curve starts
   calm and ends "almost quiet."

Grammar obligations accepted: no fixed nav bar (margin folio instead), title-page
hero with no media above the fold, hard ground cuts between chapters (painted
per section, no drift interpolation), media in its own column with captions,
close as a colophon with the CTA as running text. Bans honored: no scrub, no
spotlight, no magnet, no pinned crossfade type acts, no centred hero copy.

### Signature move (bespoke, engine untouched)

**The margin instrument: DE's technology map living beside the folio.** A
persistent margin rail (top strip on small screens) carries the chapter folio
and a miniature 14-domain system map. It scatters while the reader is in the
fragmentation chapter, snaps into one connected lattice during the peak (in
sync with the full-page figure drawing itself), becomes the reader's record for
the rest of the page, and in the Outcomes chapter the full-size map answers
input: choosing a business outcome lights the exact domains that deliver it,
in the chapter figure and in the margin instrument at once. Implemented as
page-bespoke JS reading each act's published `--sc-p`; the engine is not
touched. Reduced motion keeps every state change, drops the travel.

   resolve. Fix applied to the page, not the brief: the peak figure gained
   scroll-height (svg to 82svh) and the system energizes at p 0.88 so the
   alive-state lands while the resolve line is up.
3. **Final measured length: 13.98 viewport-heights at 1440×900** (budget 8-14,
   outside the 13.6-13.8 band). Harness: no dead scroll, all cues peak, all
   composited contrast ≥ 4.5:1, at 1440, 768, 390, and reduced-motion.
